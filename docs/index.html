Why You'll Love Pigeon

⚡

Lightning Fast

One click from your Mac menu bar, one tap from your iPhone Lock Screen. No heavy app to launch.

⏰

Schedule & Send Later

Write now, send later. Schedule emails for the perfect time with our built-in scheduler.

↩️

Undo Send

Made a typo? No problem. Configurable undo window gives you time to catch mistakes.

✈️

Works Offline

Lost connection? Your emails queue up and send automatically when you're back online.

⭐

Smart Favorites

Quick access to your most-emailed contacts. Learns from your habits automatically.

🎵

Satisfying Sounds

Choose from 13 delightful system sounds for that satisfying "sent" confirmation.

📱

iOS + Lock Screen Widget

Full iPhone app with a Lock Screen widget. Tap to compose without even unlocking.

📤

Share Extension

Share text or links from any iOS app directly into a Pigeon Mail compose sheet.

✍️

Markdown Formatting

Write in Markdown with a handy toolbar — bold, italics, links, and lists — then preview before you send.

💾

Never Lose a Draft

Every message auto-saves to Gmail as you type. Pick up right where you left off from the drafts drawer.

📎

Attach Anything

Drop in files of any type — they ride along with your message, encoded and delivered straight through Gmail.

🔍

Smart Contact Search

Start typing and Pigeon finds the right person from your Google Contacts, ranked so the best match comes first.

⌨️

Global Hotkey

Press ⌘⌥⇧M from any app to pop open a compose window instantly — no clicking required.

Get Started in Minutes

1

Clone & Build

Clone the repo from GitHub and open in Xcode. Build and run!

2

Add Your Google OAuth

Create OAuth credentials in Google Cloud Console and add your Client ID in Settings.

3

Sign In & Go

Authenticate with Google and start sending emails from your menu bar or iPhone!

Requirements

💻 macOS 14.0+
📱 iOS 17.0+
🛠️ Xcode 15+
🔑 Google Cloud OAuth
